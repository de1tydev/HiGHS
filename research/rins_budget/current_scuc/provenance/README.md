# Historical records

These retained files describe earlier immutable source or publication states.
Their original hashes, counts and pending/unpublished/unlaunched flags are
historical. The current source is identified by ../current_scuc/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json;
current observed release/panel coverage is described by ../VALIDATION.md and
../SEED_PANEL.md. Historical relative links retain their original context and
are not promises that that historical directory layout exists here.

- BASE_* records: initial portable extraction and its original attribution
- RESOURCE_PRELAUNCH_* and RESOURCE_V2_* records: the resource revision and
  its earlier prelaunch/publication snapshots, including the prior June result
- SEED_PREPUBLICATION_* records: immutable seed-v3 source inventory and the
  implementation-time README, revision and validation narratives
- ../RESOURCE_REVISION.json and ../SEED_REVISION.json: labeled historical
  records with original fields and hashes retained; private receipt paths are
  replaced by receipt names and the transformations are explicitly annotated
- ../evidence/june-cli-result.json and ../evidence/prelaunch-*: prior resource-v2
  evidence, outside the new six-run seed-sensitivity panel
