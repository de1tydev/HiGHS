The projected candidate passed the declared 1% hard-zero SCUC target with
checked upper **12,019,778.55028609**, fresh exact projected-LP lower

## Candidate and certificate scope

All 28,080 original u/y/z binaries, including fixed binaries, passed without
rounding, with zero residual. All 48,780 shedding/reserve values were exactly
zero, without repair. Positive shared overflow was
1.022330252453685e-6 MWh, below the frozen 1e-5 MWh per-category guard. Its
original 0.005111651262268425 penalty remains in the upper; zero overflow
is not claimed.

The complete literal scan covered 103,104 signed normal rows and 132,720,336
signed security rows. Both direct-DC reports covered all 1,288 source-listed
outages and 66,360,168 eligible pair-hours, with no reported post-slack
violation. This covers listed outages, not all 1,991 lines as outages.
Original-source feasibility and original-cost checks passed.

The exact weak-duality lower is for the stored binary64 projected/canonical
literal LODF hard-zero integer subset, using the frozen projection/support and
complete-family validity argument. Physical feasibility and the upper remain
numerical witnesses under inherited tolerances. This is not an exact
physical-DC lower certificate, a bound for the original soft-shedding optimum,
or a bound for the larger negligible-shedding domain. Only 22,356 originally
positive shedding upper bounds were tightened to zero; 26,388 other shedding
bounds and all 36 reserve-shortfall bounds were already zero.

Two fresh seed LPs preceded one cold discovery MIP. The seed process took
75.267010015 seconds and the MIP process took 9.734159499 seconds. The final
exact lower remains attached to the second seed's one-installed-cut-batch
matrix; the MIP receives two fresh batches. Its reported discovery lower is
excluded even though native status is Optimal. No historical points, bounds,
cuts, factors, starts, basis or search state are imported. Carry and
discovery-to-proof admission were not exercised because the first MIP point
sufficed.

The complete first-start inventory has 5,580 logical prefixes: 5,544 positive
rows for 154 units and 36 zero-cost prefixes for one additional eligible unit,
certified redundant from original bounds. It adds no columns. The generic
zero-cost rule predates December unchanged; it was not selected from this
case's numerical results. Audit verified 27 mechanical case rebindings and
all 985 frozen candidate source files without scientific retuning.

## Why the conventional run did not complete

The reference retains original angles, flows, costs and all source binaries.
It starts with an empty security cache and uses ordinary proof without seed
LPs, first-start rows, projected cuts or carry. Its policy permits at most
three cold proof calls; another call requires newly witnessed original
security rows. The first call requests 595 native seconds within the
600-second contained-process limit.

That solve returned cleanly at its native time limit. The master objective
12,131,487.963472161 and printed 2.02% gap are diagnostic only. Current-master
checks, all 28,080 binaries and the hard-zero/slack guards passed; maximum
binary residual was 1e-13, and positive overflow was zero. The complete literal
scan nevertheless found a conservative maximum security violation of
273.48958130256034 MW at zero-based hour 18, line l1202, against the unchanged
1e-5 tolerance. The new pair is monitored index 1201 and outage index 830.

The controller appended 72 original rows for that pair over all 36 hours and
both signs. Only 2.333707044 process seconds remained, below the frozen
5-second launch margin, so those rows were never solved. The final source and
direct-DC worker correctly remained unrun. No claim is made that every
intermediate incumbent was checked. The lower 11,885,856.811731141 is an
admitted conservative numerical MIP bound after the printed-bound allowance,
not an exact certificate. No full-target upper or interval is available.

The raw outcome is `actual_or_whole_time_censoring`: the contained-process
budget prevents continuation, while the 1,800-second whole limit is not
exhausted. The native log displays 595.51 seconds versus 595 requested; the
measured 597.666292956-second contained process stays within its 600-second
budget. All recorded processes were cleanly reaped, with no watchdog kill or
surviving process.

Raw diagnostics are retained. The candidate and reference respectively report
`Inconsistent max bound violation` differences of 7.276e-12 and 1.095e-8, and
the reference has the expected time-limit warning. No acceptance threshold
was added or relaxed, and warning-free execution is not claimed.

## Method, clocks and exposure

Both arms use unmodified official HiGHS commit
[`d547a3ad8af5399651187fb0e133cf0e42615b82`](https://github.com/ERGO-Code/HiGHS/commit/d547a3ad8af5399651187fb0e133cf0e42615b82).
They target the same pinned original source and hard-zero subset. This is an
application/formulation and allocation-policy comparison: the candidate
combines network projection, complete source-valid first-start cuts and fresh
network-value cuts, while the reference uses ordinary original-DC proof.
It does not isolate a solver-kernel patch or one mechanism.

Each whole clock begins immediately before process launch from the same
pinned serialized original MPS and source data and ends at exact reap. It
includes fresh transformation/factors, export/readback, validation, local
checkpoint writes, shutdown and cleanup. The candidate's 153.903346725-second
physical-check process is already included in its whole figure. Contained
ledgers measure subprocess wall time, not native solver-only time. Remote
upload/readback occurs after exit and is measured separately; its recorded
administrative endpoint precedes final archive-pointer fsync. The owner reports
a transient execution-transport outage before any reference upload intent;
the unchanged start, boot identity and clock interval are independently bound.
That delay belongs only to the separate administration clock. Acquisition,
common input generation/prevalidation, setup/build and later independent
audit are outside both arm clocks. Prepared retained models are verification
authority, not reused timed optimization state.

December was preselected for the first prospective numerical validation
evidenced by this frozen release. Source-only metadata/configuration and
preparation exposure before launch are disclosed. Local records cannot prove
absence of every unrecorded outside exposure. June remains an exposed
development case; October remains case-exposed validation after its retained
infrastructure failure and saved-point diagnosis. The previous October
report's statement that December was unopened is historical as of that report.

Candidate audit passed 2,967 recorded assertions plus separate cloud-addendum
checks. The conventional local audit passed 4,687 assertions across source,
artifact, checkpoint and terminal evidence, followed by a separate passing
cloud addendum. Auditors read and rebound retained evidence without rerunning
optimization or physical evaluation. Recorded master readback row-bound hash
differences are solely negative-zero to positive-zero normalization; the
normalized expected hashes match the loaded hashes. Other pre-publication
metadata labels remain preserved with separately verified certificate/readback
records, as detailed in the audits. Archive hashes identify retained evidence;
they are not public retrieval links. Private archive identifiers, full matrices
and full vectors are not included in the [compact evidence](evidence/scuc_december_v1.json).

The [three-case comparison](SCUC_THREE_CASE_RESULTS.md) preserves these scope
and timing differences. Independent code parity and small relocated portability
tests are separate engineering evidence; the historical full cases were not
run through the new portable CLI. No full-size portable-CLI validation, stable
or general MILP acceleration, multiseed result or repeatability is established.
Prior negative/interrupted results and correctness advisories remain unchanged.
